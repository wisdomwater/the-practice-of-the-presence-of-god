**What if the ordinary moments of your life could become places of communion with God?**

In the noise of a busy kitchen, Brother Lawrence discovered a way of prayer that accompanied everything he did. Preparing meals, fulfilling responsibilities, and beginning again after distraction became opportunities to turn toward God with simple affection.

*The Practice of the Presence of God: A 60-Day Journey into Divine Awareness, Simplicity, and Love* brings his wisdom into contemporary language through retellings of four conversations and fifteen letters, accompanied by sixty original devotional readings.

Each daily reflection includes a gentle practice and a brief breath prayer, inviting you to explore divine companionship within work, rest, relationships, and uncertainty. Along the way, you will encounter themes of trust, humility, perseverance, and the freedom to return without becoming discouraged by your imperfections.

Rooted in Christian contemplation, this book welcomes those seeking a quieter, more attentive faith—one that makes room for honest questions and the difficulties of being human.

Whether you are new to contemplative prayer or longing to deepen an established practice, these pages invite you to recognize the possibilities for love already present within your day.

Begin with the life you have. Bring your wandering attention, your unfinished hopes, and your willingness to be loved. There is room for prayer here.
