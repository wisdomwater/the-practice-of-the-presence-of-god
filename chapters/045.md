## Day 45 - Attention Becomes Affection

We often discover affection through the simple act of paying attention. Someone's familiar gesture becomes endearing, a place grows precious through repeated visits, or a conversation reveals a kindness we had previously missed. As we come to know more, we find ourselves wanting to return and give more of our attention.

Brother Lawrence describes this movement in our relationship with God. We remember God in order to know Him, and growing love then draws our thoughts toward Him more readily. Attention and affection begin to nourish one another, gradually shaping what the heart regards as treasure.

At first, remembrance may depend upon a deliberate choice. We pause before work, give thanks for a meal, or bring a concern into prayer because we want the relationship to deepen. There may be little immediate feeling, yet these encounters create room for familiarity to grow.

Over time, we may notice that prayer comes to mind through gratitude or that an ordinary experience awakens a desire to share it with God. We can receive these movements without trying to force them. The repeated practice becomes an ongoing relationship, with each small act of attention offering another opportunity to know and to love.

**Practice**

Choose one ordinary experience you would naturally want to share with someone close to you. It could be something beautiful, frustrating, amusing, or uncertain. Bring it into a brief conversation with God, using the language you actually think in.

Then pause to consider what your prayer reveals about the relationship you are seeking. Perhaps you long to be understood, guided, accompanied, or able to give thanks. Let that longing remain present for a few moments. Later, if something reminds you of the conversation, respond naturally. Allow deliberate attention and spontaneous remembrance to have their own place as familiarity gradually develops.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *Let me know You more.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *Let my love grow deep.*
