## Day 36 - Without Knowing What Comes Next

We often hope that a sense of purpose will arrive with a clear explanation of what comes next. We want to understand where our work is leading, what a difficult season will produce, or why our lives have taken a particular shape. Without that understanding, we may feel as though we are waiting to begin living fully.

Brother Lawrence admits that he does not know what God intends for him or why his life continues. Alongside that uncertainty, he describes a profound calm rooted in God's company. He has found a place to stand even though the future remains beyond his understanding.

His words leave room for a faith that can speak honestly about its limits. We may continue asking questions, seeking counsel, and making decisions with the knowledge available to us. We can also allow our present relationships and responsibilities to matter before we understand how they fit into a larger story.

Perhaps today offers no explanation, but it does offer someone to listen to, a task worth completing, or a need we can meet. We can ask God to accompany those responses. Over time, a meaningful life may take shape through such participation, while much of its direction remains hidden from our view.

**Practice**

Name one question about your future that you cannot presently answer. Notice what you hope an answer would give you: security, permission to act, reassurance, or a sense of meaning. Bring that longing into prayer without requiring yourself to resolve it.

Then identify one thing that deserves your care today, regardless of how the larger question unfolds. Give it a modest, concrete response. You might prepare something needed, contact someone, or complete a manageable task. When uncertainty returns, remind yourself that this act of care remains worthwhile. Allow God's companionship to be part of the life you can live now.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *You are with me now.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *Guide me as life unfolds.*
