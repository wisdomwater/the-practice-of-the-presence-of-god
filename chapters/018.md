## Day 18 - Beneath Our Imperfect Efforts

When a helpful habit begins to take root, we naturally look back at what we did to establish it. We remember the effort, the repeated decisions, and the occasions when we continued despite discouragement. Gratitude for that perseverance can mingle with a growing pressure to keep everything going through our own strength.

Brother Lawrence acknowledged both the difficulty of his practice and the imperfection with which he followed it. Yet he attributed its benefits entirely to God's mercy and goodness. Looking back, he saw a generosity that had accompanied his efforts and exceeded what he could claim to have accomplished.

This perspective can soften the way we understand spiritual growth. Our willingness matters, and the small choices we make can help a relationship deepen. Alongside those choices, we can learn to recognize what we receive: an unexpected desire to pray, encouragement from another person, or the strength to begin again after a weary day.

Brother Lawrence ended his account by inviting someone else to give thanks with him. His wonder opened outward into shared gratitude. We too can pause within our unfinished growth and acknowledge the goodness already reaching us, allowing thanksgiving to nourish the practice we are still learning.

**Practice**

Look back over the past few days for one small gift that supported your spiritual life. Perhaps someone listened without judgment, a passage stayed with you, or you found yourself willing to pray after a period of distraction. Choose something particular rather than trying to assess your overall progress.

Give thanks for that gift and for whatever helped make it possible. You may also wish to thank a person whose care has sustained you. Then consider one modest way to remain open to similar help today. Let gratitude accompany your next step, especially where your own energy or confidence feels limited.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *Your goodness sustains me.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *I receive it with thanks.*
