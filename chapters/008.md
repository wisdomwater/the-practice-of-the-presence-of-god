## Day 8 - Loving Within Uncertainty

When something important feels uncertain, our minds often return to it, searching for an answer that will finally let us rest. We rehearse possibilities, examine our feelings, and revisit questions we have already considered. Sometimes the search occupies so much of our attention that little remains for the life unfolding before us.

Brother Lawrence described four years of anguish during which he believed himself condemned. His eventual resolution was deeply personal: whatever awaited him, he would spend his remaining life loving God. Within a question he could not resolve, he discovered a response he could still offer.

We can receive something from his experience while recognizing that such anguish deserves companionship and care. His story invites us to consider how love might remain possible even when reassurance feels beyond our reach. A conversation, an honest prayer, or an act of kindness can give us somewhere to place our attention while uncertainty remains.

There may be questions we carry for a long time, including questions about God, ourselves, and what the future holds. We can bring them into prayer and seek whatever understanding or support is available. Alongside that search, we can allow today's opportunities for love to receive us back into the present, where a life of communion is already possible.

**Practice**

Notice one question you have been repeatedly trying to settle. Write it down in a single sentence, then consider whether there is a useful step available today. You might seek information, speak with someone you trust, or arrange time for a needed conversation.

If no further step is available now, gently acknowledge that limit. Pray, “God, accompany me while this remains unresolved.” Then choose one small expression of love within reach: listen to someone, care for your surroundings, or receive a moment of rest. Allow the unanswered question to remain present without requiring it to occupy your whole attention.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *Be with me here.*
> **Breathing out:** *Help me love today.*
