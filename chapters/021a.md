## Third Letter — A Heart Given to God

*To the Reverend Mother*  
*Undated*

My Reverend and Greatly Honored Mother,

Today I received two books and a letter from a sister who is preparing to make her religious vows. As she approaches this commitment, she asks for the prayers of your community and especially for yours. She clearly places great confidence in them, so please do not disappoint her. Ask God that she may offer herself entirely for His love, with a firm determination to belong wholly to Him.

I will send you one of the books, which concerns the practice of God's presence. In my understanding, this practice embraces the whole spiritual life. It seems to me that anyone who faithfully undertakes it will soon grow in the life of the Spirit.

To practice it rightly, I believe the heart must be emptied of everything else, because God desires to possess it entirely. Unless it is free of all besides Him, He cannot possess it alone. We must leave the heart open and wholly available to Him so that He may accomplish within it what He desires.

I know of no way of life sweeter or more delightful than continually walking with God. Only those who practice it and experience it for themselves can fully understand its joy. Yet I would not advise you to undertake it for the pleasure it brings. Let us do so out of love, and because God desires us to live in His company.

If I were a preacher, the practice of God's presence would be the subject I would commend above everything else. If I were a spiritual director, I would recommend it to everyone. So necessary do I consider it, and so simple to undertake.

If we truly understood how deeply we need God's grace and assistance, we would never willingly lose sight of Him, even for a moment. I urge you, then, to make a holy and firm resolution this very instant: never again deliberately to turn away from Him. Resolve to spend the remainder of your life in His holy presence, willing, for His love, to surrender every other pleasure should He ask it of you.

Give yourself wholeheartedly to this practice. If you follow it faithfully, I am confident that you will soon begin to experience its effects. I will support you with my prayers, poor though they are, and I earnestly ask for yours and those of your holy community.

Yours in their service, and especially in yours.
