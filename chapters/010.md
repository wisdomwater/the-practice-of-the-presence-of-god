## Day 10 - Enough Light for Today

Before a difficult conversation has begun, we may have already lived through it several times. We imagine objections, rehearse our explanations, and prepare for outcomes that may never arrive. Some preparation helps us respond wisely, but there comes a point when anticipation consumes the attention we need for the life actually before us.

Brother Lawrence remembered being troubled by considerable anxiety about his responsibilities. Over time, repeated experiences of God's assistance changed how he approached them. When the moment for action arrived, he found in God, as in a clear mirror, enough understanding to recognize what he should do.

His experience invites us to consider how trust might shape our preparation. We can gather information, make arrangements, and acknowledge the limits of what we know. Having done what is presently useful, we can entrust the coming encounter to God without continuing to rehearse every possibility.

The next step may become clear only when we meet the person, hear the question, or discover the actual difficulty. Until then, we can remain available to what this hour asks of us. The God we hope to encounter tomorrow is already here, accompanying the work and relationships that deserve our attention today.

**Practice**

Choose one approaching responsibility that has been occupying your thoughts. Write down whatever preparation would genuinely help, and take one practical step toward completing it. Then notice which remaining concerns depend on information or circumstances you do not yet have.

Offer those uncertainties to God in a simple prayer: “Help me recognize what is needed when the time comes.” Return your attention to something presently within your care. If the anticipated situation comes to mind again, gently ask whether any new action is available. When there is nothing further to do, let yourself resume the task or conversation before you.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *You are with me here.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *Guide my next step.*
