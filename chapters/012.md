## Day 12 - The Love Our Practices Serve

A spiritual practice can begin as a welcome invitation and gradually become something we feel compelled to maintain. We count the days we have prayed, the chapters we have read, or the comforts we have surrendered. These habits may support us, yet we can become so occupied with keeping them that we lose sight of the relationship they were meant to nourish.

Brother Lawrence believed that many people became absorbed in religious exercises while neglecting the love of God toward which those exercises should lead. He looked for their fruit in the way people lived. Effort and sacrifice mattered because of what they helped a person become capable of receiving and expressing.

This invites a gentle examination of our own habits. We might consider whether prayer is making us more willing to listen, whether silence is helping us respond with patience, or whether self-denial is creating room for generosity. Such questions can bring us back into contact with the purpose that first drew us toward a practice.

Some disciplines may need patient perseverance; others may need adjustment as our circumstances change. We can ask for guidance without making our spiritual routine another measure of personal worth. Beneath its changing forms, the invitation remains to offer God a willing heart and allow love to shape our conduct.

**Practice**

Choose one spiritual habit you currently observe, or one you have been meaning to begin. Recall what first drew you toward it. Consider how it has affected your attention to God and your treatment of other people, allowing yourself to notice both its gifts and its difficulties.

Ask, “How might this practice help me become more available to love?” You may sense an invitation to continue, simplify, or approach it with a different intention. Make one gentle adjustment if needed. Then give the practice a concrete expression in your day through listening, generosity, patience, or care for someone nearby.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *Draw my heart toward You.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *Let love shape my life.*
