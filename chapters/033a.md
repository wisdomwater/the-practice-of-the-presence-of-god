## Seventh Letter — The Treasure Without End

*To the Reverend Mother*  
*Undated*

My Reverend and Greatly Honored Mother,

You may count upon my prayers, little worth though they may have. I have promised to pray for you, and I will keep my word. How happy we could be if we discovered the treasure of which the Gospel speaks. Everything else would seem as nothing beside it.

Its riches are infinite. The more deeply we search within this treasure, the more we discover. Let us therefore persevere in seeking it, without growing weary or giving up before we have found it.

*[After addressing some private matters, Brother Lawrence continues.]*

I do not know what will become of me. It seems that peace of soul and rest of spirit continue to settle upon me even while I sleep. To lose the awareness of this peace would be a real affliction. Yet with such calm within my soul, I believe I could find consolation even in purgatory.

I do not know what God intends for me or for what purpose He keeps me here. My peace is so great that I fear nothing. What could I fear while I am with Him? I remain in His presence as much as I am able.

May all things praise Him. Amen.

Yours in our Lord.
