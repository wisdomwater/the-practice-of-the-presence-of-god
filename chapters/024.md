## Day 24 - Choosing the One We Love

At the beginning of a friendship, we often notice how good another person's company makes us feel. We look forward to the ease, laughter, or understanding we find together. As the relationship deepens, we also learn to remain present when our companion is tired, conversation is difficult, or being together asks something of us.

Brother Lawrence describes walking with God as the sweetest and most delightful way of life. Yet he cautions against undertaking the practice for its pleasures. He urges his reader to choose God's company out of love, willing to relinquish other pleasures if faithfulness should require it.

This invitation reaches into the decisions through which affection becomes real. We might interrupt our entertainment to listen, release the satisfaction of winning an argument, or acknowledge something we would prefer to hide. In these moments, love asks us to consider what matters beyond our immediate enjoyment.

We can bring the same sincerity to our relationship with God. We welcome the joy it brings while also becoming willing to receive correction, offer attention, and follow where love leads. Our commitment takes shape through these ordinary choices, as we gradually learn to cherish the One whose company we first sought for comfort.

**Practice**

Notice one moment today when a loving response asks you to relinquish a small pleasure. Perhaps you could put down your phone to listen, give up the final word in a disagreement, or pause something enjoyable to meet a genuine responsibility.

Before responding, acknowledge what you would prefer and what the situation appears to need. Ask God for help in choosing freely, then make the modest offering available to you. Afterward, notice what you learned without judging the action by how rewarding it felt. Allow the choice to express affection, even if it remains uncomfortable or passes without acknowledgment.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *God, deepen my love.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *I offer You my willingness.*
