## Day 16 - Finding a Simple Way

We can spend a great deal of time looking for the right way to grow spiritually. A book introduces one practice, a teacher recommends another, and soon we have gathered more possibilities than we can carry. Our sincere desire for God becomes entangled with the worry that we might be overlooking something essential.

Brother Lawrence recognized this difficulty in himself. The many methods he encountered seemed likely to confuse him, while his deepest desire remained simple: he wanted to belong wholly to God. He chose a way he could carry into his actual life, returning his attention to the One he desired to love.

There is wisdom in noticing what helps us become available to God. A practice may be valuable and still exceed our present capacity, or require circumstances we rarely have. We can receive guidance with gratitude while allowing our prayer to take a form we can inhabit honestly.

Perhaps, for this season, we need a few quiet minutes, a familiar prayer, or a brief pause before beginning work. We can give that small opening time to deepen. As we grow accustomed to meeting God there, we may discover that a simple practice can hold more of our longing than we expected.

**Practice**

Consider the spiritual practices you currently attempt or feel you ought to maintain. Notice which one helps you approach God with honesty and openness, and which expectations leave you preoccupied with keeping up.

Choose one simple practice to receive your attention for the next few days. It might be a short prayer upon waking, a moment of silence at lunch, or an evening expression of gratitude. Let it fit the life you actually have. When you practice, bring your desire for God with you, however faint or uncertain it feels. Allow familiarity to develop without adding further requirements.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *God, receive my longing.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *Lead me in simplicity.*
