## Day 47 - Loving Without Holding Too Tightly

The people we love become woven into our sense of safety and belonging. Their presence reassures us, their understanding helps us know ourselves, and their affection gives ordinary days a particular warmth. When a relationship changes or is lost, we may discover how much of our inner stability depended upon its familiar shape.

Brother Lawrence writes about a man who has suffered the loss of a friend. He wonders whether the attachment had become too consuming and urges a deeper confidence in God. His counsel raises a delicate question about how we love others while remembering that no person can carry the whole weight of our security.

We can explore that question tenderly, allowing grief its full place. A relationship's absence may hurt precisely because something precious was shared. Trust in God can hold that sorrow while helping us recognize where affection has become entangled with the need to control, possess, or receive constant reassurance.

As we grow in trust, we may become more able to cherish another person's freedom and accept the limits of what a relationship can provide. We can remain grateful for love received, seek support when loss unsettles us, and ask God to help our affection mature into a generous and spacious care.

**Practice**

Bring to mind someone whose presence matters deeply to you. Give thanks for one particular gift you receive through the relationship. Then gently notice whether there is something you expect this person to provide that exceeds their capacity or freedom.

Offer that expectation to God without condemning yourself for having it. Ask what a more generous response might look like today: allowing space, making a clear request, or receiving support from more than one source. If you are grieving someone's absence, let remembrance and sorrow be your prayer. There is no need to hurry toward an explanation or a new attachment.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *Hold the ones I love.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *Make my love more generous.*
