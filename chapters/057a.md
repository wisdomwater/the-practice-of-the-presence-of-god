## Fifteenth Letter — A Heart Made into a Temple

*To the Reverend Mother*  
*January 22, 1691*

My Very Dear Mother,

I give thanks to our Lord for granting you some of the relief you desired. I myself have often been close to death, yet I have never felt more deeply content than at those times. I did not ask for relief, but prayed instead for the strength to suffer courageously, gently, and lovingly.

How sweet it is to suffer in God's company! However great your suffering may be, receive it with love. To suffer while remaining with Him is paradise to me. If we wish to experience something of heaven's peace even in this life, let us become accustomed to a familiar, humble, loving conversation with God.

Let us keep our attention from wandering away from Him and make our hearts into a spiritual temple where we offer Him continual worship. We must watch over our conduct, our words, and our thoughts, seeking to avoid anything that might displease Him. When our minds are filled with God in this way, suffering itself can become infused with sweetness, spiritual consolation, and quiet joy.

I know that the beginning of this way of life is very difficult, because we must proceed by faith alone. Yet however demanding it may seem, we also know that God's grace enables us to do all things. He never refuses that grace to those who ask Him earnestly.

Knock, and persevere in knocking. I assure you that He will open to you in His own time. In a single moment, He can grant what He has withheld for many years.

Farewell. Pray for me, as I pray for you. I hope to see Him very soon.

Entirely yours in our Lord.
