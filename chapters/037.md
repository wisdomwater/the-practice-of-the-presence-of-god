## Day 37 - The Help Already Arriving

When we are waiting for relief, we naturally look for the circumstance itself to change. We hope the conflict will end, the burden will lift, or an answer will finally arrive. Until then, it can seem as though help has been delayed and we have been left to manage alone.

Brother Lawrence directs his correspondent's attention toward the courage and patience she has received amid her afflictions. He regards these as signs of God's care already reaching her. While she continues to hope for assistance, he invites her to recognize the strength that is helping her endure.

We can receive this invitation without needing to call our suffering good or explain why it has happened. There may still be something we need to resist, change, or grieve. Alongside that honest response, we can notice the particular forms of support that have kept us from facing everything entirely alone.

Perhaps someone has listened, an unexpected interval has allowed us to rest, or enough courage has appeared for one necessary conversation. Gratitude can begin with these modest gifts. As we acknowledge them, we make room to receive more help and to trust that care can reach us within a difficulty whose ending we cannot yet see.

**Practice**

Bring to mind one difficulty that remains unresolved. Name what you wish would change, allowing that desire to be part of your prayer. Then consider what has helped you bear the situation so far.

Look for something particular: a person's kindness, a practical resource, a moment of rest, or the willingness to ask for assistance. Give thanks for that support without requiring yourself to feel grateful for the suffering itself. Consider whether there is one further form of help you could receive today. Ask for it if you can, allowing gratitude and continued need to remain together.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *Your care reaches me.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *Help me receive it.*
