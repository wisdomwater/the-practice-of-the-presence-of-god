## Day 51 - At the Table with Our Doubts

We can receive kindness and still wonder whether we deserve to remain. Someone welcomes us, yet we remember a failure they may not know about. Affection reaches us, while part of our attention stays fixed upon the possibility that a fuller knowledge of us would change the welcome.

Brother Lawrence describes something similar at his King's table. He imagines being personally served while remaining troubled by his sins and uncertain of pardon. His letter holds delight and anguish together, revealing how an experience of divine generosity can coexist with a heart still learning to trust it.

There is tenderness in recognizing this tension, even in someone whose prayer seems so deeply established. We may have assumed that genuine faith would settle every fear or that receiving love would immediately quiet self-accusation. His honesty gives us room to acknowledge the reassurance we need without concealing the goodness we have already received.

We can bring our doubts to the table as well. Where a wrong needs repair, we can respond responsibly; where fear keeps repeating an accusation, we can ask for help in understanding it. Meanwhile, we can practice receiving one kindness without first resolving every question about our worthiness to be loved.

**Practice**

Recall a recent kindness that you found difficult to receive. Notice what arose alongside it: gratitude, suspicion, embarrassment, or an impulse to repay it immediately. Bring those responses honestly before God.

Imagine sitting at the table Brother Lawrence describes, with your uncertainty still present. Allow yourself to remain there for a few moments without preparing a defense. If a specific regret comes to mind, consider whether it calls for repair or a conversation with someone you trust. Then return to the offered kindness. Practice acknowledging it with a simple “thank you,” allowing generosity to reach you while you are still learning to trust.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *Your kindness reaches me.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *Help me remain and receive.*
