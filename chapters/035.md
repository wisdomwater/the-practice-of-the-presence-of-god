## Day 35 - While We Sleep

At the end of a demanding day, we may lie down while our minds continue working. We review what remains unfinished, revisit our mistakes, and prepare for tomorrow's difficulties. Even rest can feel like something we must supervise, as though letting go of attention might allow our lives to slip beyond repair.

Brother Lawrence writes that peace of soul seems to descend upon him even in sleep. He speaks with wonder about a gift continuing when his deliberate efforts have ceased. His recollection invites us to consider how God's care might exceed the limits of our conscious attention.

We cannot arrange for every night to feel peaceful, and troubled sleep is no measure of our faith. Yet we can begin to loosen the assumption that our relationship with God depends upon constant inward activity. The One to whom we pray remains present when words end and the body needs rest.

Preparing for sleep can therefore include a small act of entrusting. We acknowledge the unfinished work, take whatever practical steps are needed, and allow the day to close. Even if our thoughts remain unsettled, we can offer our tiredness to God and receive rest as part of the life we share with Him.

**Practice**

As your day draws toward its end, write down one matter you need to remember tomorrow. If helpful, add the next practical step so that you do not have to keep rehearsing it. Then set the note aside.

Offer a brief prayer for whatever remains unfinished: “God, receive what I cannot complete tonight.” Notice the support beneath your body and allow yourself a few comfortable breaths. You do not need to produce calm or make sleep happen. If concerns return, acknowledge them gently and remember that you have made provision to revisit them. Let resting be your present responsibility.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *Your care surrounds me.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *I entrust this night to You.*
