## Day 55 - When Suffering Continues

At the beginning of another person's difficulty, we may find it easy to offer attention. We ask questions, remember important dates, and hope that relief will come soon. When suffering continues, however, we can become unsure what to say, especially when our earlier encouragement has changed so little.

Brother Lawrence opens his fourteenth letter by expressing sorrow that his correspondent has suffered for so long. Before offering his understanding of her affliction, he lets her know that its duration matters to him. Her pain has remained present within his concern.

His words invite us to consider the importance of care that continues beyond the first conversation. Someone living with prolonged difficulty may need to tell the same story again or acknowledge a disappointment we hoped had passed. Listening requires a willingness to remain close without insisting upon a new development.

We can also receive this invitation when we are the ones still hurting. The passage of time does not make our need for companionship less worthy of attention. We may ask someone to sit with us, hear what remains difficult, or help with a recurring task. Such care gives enduring sorrow a place within relationship, where it can be acknowledged without being hurried toward a conclusion.

**Practice**

Think of someone whose difficulty has continued beyond the period when people first offered support. If appropriate, contact them with a simple acknowledgment: “I remember that this is still part of your life. How are you doing today?”

Listen for what they actually share, including anything you have heard before. Offer one practical kindness you can sustain, rather than a general promise of availability. If you are carrying a prolonged burden yourself, consider telling someone what remains difficult. You might ask for a listening ear or help with a specific task. Allow continuing need to receive continuing care.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *You know what still hurts.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *Keep us faithful in care.*
