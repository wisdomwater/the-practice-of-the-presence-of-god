## Day 57 - Beyond What We Can Imagine

When help does not arrive as we hoped, our picture of love can begin to contract. We wonder whether we have asked too much, trusted too readily, or become a burden. The unanswered need starts to shape what we believe we are allowed to expect from God.

Near the end of his fourteenth letter, Brother Lawrence tells his correspondent that God's love exceeds everything she can imagine. Alongside his demanding counsel about surrender, this assurance gives his letter its deepest tenderness. He wants her to understand herself as someone loved beyond the limits of her present comprehension.

We may need to hold that assurance while questioning other parts of his advice. His recommendation to abandon remedies need not become our response; seeking treatment and accepting skilled care can belong within a life of trust. We can bring both our hope and our discernment into the relationship he encourages.

Perhaps we begin by allowing the possibility that our current experience does not tell us everything about divine love. We can ask questions, seek relief, and admit disappointment while remaining open to care. Our understanding may be incomplete, yet we can offer it honestly and ask God to meet us beyond the boundaries fear has drawn around our expectations.

**Practice**

Complete this sentence privately: “Because this has happened, I am afraid that God…” Let yourself name the conclusion that troubles you, without judging it or rushing to replace it.

Read Brother Lawrence's assurance slowly: God's love reaches beyond what we can imagine. Notice whether the words awaken comfort, resistance, longing, or uncertainty. Bring that response into prayer. Then choose one available act of care to receive today, including professional help if that is what you need. You do not have to settle the larger question first. Allow a small opening for kindness within the understanding you presently have.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *Your love exceeds my knowing.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *Help me stay open to care.*
