## Day 15 - Among the Noise and Clatter

A busy room can make our attention feel scattered before we have begun. Someone asks a question while another task remains unfinished, a device sounds, and we feel ourselves hurrying inwardly. We imagine that peace will become possible once everyone is satisfied and the demands have finally stopped.

Brother Lawrence described remaining with God amid the noise and clatter of the monastery kitchen. Several people might call for different things at once, yet he experienced a tranquility comparable to kneeling before the Blessed Sacrament. This steadiness had grown through years of offering his work, asking for help, and renewing his attention after distraction.

His example gives us a patient horizon for our own practice. We may begin with a single breath before responding or a brief acknowledgment that we cannot attend to everything simultaneously. Giving one task its proper attention can become a small expression of trust within circumstances that remain demanding.

There will also be moments when we need rest, assistance, or fewer responsibilities. We can bring those needs into our communion with God as readily as the work itself. Gradually, we may discover room for a quieter response within the activity of the day, allowing God's companionship to accompany us while the kitchen is still busy.

**Practice**

When several demands compete for your attention today, notice the impulse to rush. If circumstances allow, pause for one comfortable breath and feel your feet against the floor. Silently acknowledge God's presence, then identify the next thing that needs your care.

Attend to that one thing as fully as you reasonably can. You may need to ask someone to wait, request assistance, or leave a less urgent task unfinished. Let those choices belong to the practice too. After the busy moment passes, notice any small difference the pause made, without judging yourself for the agitation that may have remained.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *Meet me in this moment.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *Steady me in Your love.*
