## Day 23 - Making Room in the Heart

There are times when we sit down to pray and discover how crowded our inner life has become. We carry unfinished conversations, plans for the coming week, and concerns that seem to demand constant attention. Even our longing for quiet can become another demand, leaving us unsure how to offer God any room.

Brother Lawrence speaks strongly about the heart being emptied of everything else so that God may possess it entirely. His language invites a searching examination of what governs our affections. We can ask which concerns have become so commanding that we struggle to respond freely to the love we desire.

Perhaps we are holding tightly to approval, a particular outcome, or the belief that we must remain in control. These attachments often gather around genuine needs and responsibilities. Bringing them before God allows us to consider how our grip upon them affects our capacity to listen, trust, and care.

Making room may begin with acknowledging one thing we are reluctant to release. We can ask for help in entrusting it to God while continuing to meet whatever responsibility belongs to us. As that willingness grows, more of our inward life becomes available for the relationship we have come to seek.

**Practice**

Spend a few quiet minutes noticing what most insistently occupies your heart. Choose one concern rather than trying to clear away everything at once. Name both what matters about it and what you fear might happen if you loosened your grip.

Offer it to God in your own words. Ask whether there is a necessary action to take, a limit to acknowledge, or an outcome you must leave unresolved for now. Then rest briefly without attempting to solve it. If the concern returns, gently bring it into the prayer again, allowing surrender to begin as a small willingness.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *Make room within me.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *I entrust this to You.*
