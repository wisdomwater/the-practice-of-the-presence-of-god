## Day 29 - A Sanctuary Within

Certain places help us become quiet almost as soon as we enter them. A church, a garden, or a familiar chair may hold memories of prayer that make attention easier. When we cannot reach such a place, we may feel cut off from the conditions we need for communion.

Brother Lawrence invites his reader to make a sanctuary within her heart. There she can turn toward God for a gentle, humble conversation, even while surrounded by ordinary activity. His image gives her a place of prayer that can accompany her wherever the day requires her to be.

We might imagine this inward sanctuary as a small room where we are free to speak honestly and listen without haste. We bring the concerns of the day with us, including the people and responsibilities that need our care. A brief inward pause can help us return to them with greater attentiveness.

Some days that room will feel crowded or difficult to enter. Brother Lawrence reminds us that God knows what each person can do. We can begin with a moment of recollection, trusting that our imperfect attention is welcome and that divine companionship is available within the circumstances we already inhabit.

**Practice**

During an ordinary pause today, imagine making room within your heart for a quiet conversation with God. You might picture a simple room, a sheltered garden, or no image at all. Choose whatever helps you become gently attentive.

Bring one concern into that space and speak about it plainly. Remain for a few breaths without requiring an answer or a change in feeling. Then return to your surroundings, noticing one person or task that deserves your attention. Later, if you remember, revisit this inward place briefly. Let it become familiar through welcome rather than through pressure to concentrate.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *Dwell within my heart.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *Help me welcome You.*
