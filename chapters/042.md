## Day 42 - What We Carry into Silence

When a busy day finally becomes quiet, the mind often continues at its earlier pace. Messages, headlines, and unfinished exchanges pass through us long after their immediate demands have ended. We may be surprised by the noise we encounter in prayer, though much of it has accompanied us throughout the day.

Brother Lawrence connects our attention during prayer with the habits we cultivate at other times. He encourages frequent remembrance of God within ordinary activity, believing that this familiarity makes recollection easier. The hours outside formal prayer help shape how we enter it.

We can explore this connection through small choices about what receives our attention. Some concerns need sustained thought, and our minds also need room to imagine and rest. Alongside these movements, there may be occasions when we fill every available pause simply because unoccupied space feels unfamiliar.

Leaving one such pause open can become a gentle preparation for prayer. We might notice our surroundings, acknowledge God's presence, or allow an experience to settle before reaching for the next input. Gradually, these intervals can help us carry a more familiar attentiveness into silence, even while much of our inward life remains unsettled and in need of patient care.

**Practice**

Choose one brief interval you usually fill automatically: waiting for water to boil, standing in line, or pausing between tasks. Let that interval remain free of additional input today.

Notice what is already around you and what is moving through your mind. Briefly remember God, then allow yourself to remain with the moment without turning it into a concentration exercise. Later, when you enter prayer, acknowledge whatever you have carried from the day. Offer one concern to God and let yourself settle as you are able. Receive both pauses as opportunities to become familiar with a quieter way of attending.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *Gather my attention.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *Let me dwell with You.*
