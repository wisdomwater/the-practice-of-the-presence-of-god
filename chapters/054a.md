## Fourteenth Letter — Entrusting Affliction to Divine Love

*To the Reverend Mother*  
*Undated*

My Good Mother,

It pains me to know that your suffering has continued so long. What brings me some consolation amid my sorrow for you is my conviction that these afflictions are signs of God's love. I believe that receiving them in this light will help you bear them more easily.

In your present circumstances, my own judgment is that you should set aside human remedies and entrust yourself entirely to God's providence. Perhaps He is waiting for this surrender and complete confidence before healing you. Since the treatments you have pursued so carefully have failed to bring the expected benefit, and your illness has instead grown worse, I do not believe you would be putting God to the test by placing yourself wholly in His hands and looking to Him for everything.

As I wrote in my last letter, I believe God sometimes permits the body to suffer in order to heal the illnesses of the soul. Take courage, then, and turn what you cannot avoid into an offering of love. Ask Him for the strength to endure your bodily pain bravely, rather than asking only for its removal. Offer to bear, for His love, whatever He wills and for as long as He permits.

Such prayers are difficult for our human nature. Yet I believe they are deeply pleasing to God and become sweet to those who love Him. Love softens suffering, and the person who loves God can find joy and courage in enduring something for His sake.

I beg you to seek this consolation in His company. He is the Physician of all our afflictions and the Father of those who suffer, always ready to come to our aid. His love for us reaches infinitely beyond anything we can imagine.

Love Him, therefore, and seek your relief in Him alone. I hope that you will soon receive it. Farewell for now; I will support you with my prayers, poor though they are.

Always yours in our Lord.
