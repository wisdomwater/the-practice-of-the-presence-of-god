## Day 31 - When Our Path Looks Different

We sometimes learn a description of spiritual growth and immediately begin searching for ourselves within it. There are stages to pass through, experiences to recognize, and signs that suggest we are progressing. When our lives fail to follow the expected pattern, we may wonder whether something essential has gone wrong.

Brother Lawrence begins his sixth letter by acknowledging that he has not found his way of life described in books. Someone has explained a progression from fear through hope to pure love, yet this account leaves him discouraged. His own relationship with God has developed differently, through a simple intention to give himself wholly to Him.

His response combines confidence with openness. He describes what he has experienced honestly, while asking a trusted priest to consider it with him. He allows his particular experience to be heard and examined without first forcing it into a pattern that does not fit.

We can approach our own spiritual lives with similar honesty. Guidance may help us recognize what we cannot see alone, while comparison may obscure the relationship already taking shape. We can ask whether our path is nurturing love, humility, and faithfulness, remaining willing to learn as we attend to the life God has actually entrusted to us.

**Practice**

Notice one expectation you carry about how spiritual growth should unfold. Perhaps you believe you should feel more certain, pray in a particular way, or have reached a recognizable stage by now. Write it down, then describe your actual experience without trying to make it fit.

Consider what is helping you become more loving and honest, and where you would welcome guidance. Bring both observations into prayer. If appropriate, share them with a trusted spiritual companion who can listen thoughtfully. Allow the conversation to deepen your understanding rather than merely confirm whether you are progressing at the expected pace.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *You know the path I walk.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *Keep me open to Your guidance.*
