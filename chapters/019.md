## Day 19 - Beyond the Passing Wave

A moment of beauty can awaken something we had almost forgotten. Music moves us, a familiar prayer becomes unexpectedly vivid, or sunlight through a window leaves us briefly grateful to be alive. We welcome these moments, yet their passing can make the rest of the day seem spiritually empty.

In his second letter, Brother Lawrence compared God's treasure to an infinite ocean. He wondered why we content ourselves with a passing wave when such abundance is available. His image invites us to consider a relationship whose depth extends beyond the moments when we feel its movement most clearly.

We naturally cherish experiences that reassure us of God's nearness. Yet we may begin searching for the feeling itself, measuring each prayer against a remembered encounter. The quieter expressions of communion become harder to recognize: a willingness to listen, an honest confession, or a small act of care offered without enthusiasm.

We can receive the wave with gratitude and allow it to pass without demanding its return. There is still room to turn toward God in the ordinary hours that follow. As our attention becomes less dependent upon a particular feeling, we may discover a steadier way of belonging within the love those beautiful moments helped us glimpse.

**Practice**

Recall a moment when you felt especially close to God, deeply grateful, or quietly aware of life's goodness. Give thanks for it without trying to recreate the experience. Notice what it awakened in you, such as tenderness, trust, wonder, or a desire to care.

Choose one simple way to give that response expression today. You might listen attentively, spend a few minutes outside, or offer an ordinary task to God. Allow the action to have its own character, even if it brings no memorable feeling. Let your earlier experience nourish your present willingness without becoming a standard the moment must meet.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *Your love is deep.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *I rest within it.*
