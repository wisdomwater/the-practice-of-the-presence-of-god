## Day 34 - A Treasure That Keeps Opening

When we first discover something meaningful, we often approach it with an attention that familiarity gradually diminishes. A landscape becomes part of the commute, a friendship settles into routine, or a cherished passage becomes words we no longer pause to hear. We assume we already know what is there and move on in search of something new.

Brother Lawrence describes a treasure whose riches increase as we explore it. The more deeply we search, the more we discover. His invitation to persevere rests upon the abundance of what remains available within our relationship with God.

This gives a different character to the familiar movements of prayer. We may return to the same words while bringing a life that has changed since we last spoke them. A sorrow, a new responsibility, or an unexpected kindness can open a meaning we were previously unable to receive.

There is room for curiosity within faithfulness. We can ask what we have overlooked, where love is inviting a fuller response, or how an ordinary moment might become an encounter. Familiarity need not exhaust the relationship; it can give us the trust to explore its depths more honestly, allowing what we have already received to become an opening toward more.

**Practice**

Return to one familiar prayer, passage, or place that has nourished your spiritual life. Approach it slowly, allowing yourself to notice a detail you usually pass over. Ask what it might offer within your present circumstances.

Stay with one word, image, or observation rather than trying to gather several insights. If nothing feels new, acknowledge that honestly and remain for a few moments. Later, consider whether the encounter invites a particular response, such as gratitude, patience, or a conversation you have delayed. Let the practice be an exploration of a relationship whose depths you do not need to exhaust today.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *Your love has hidden depths.*
>
> **Breathing out:** *Teach me to receive more.*
