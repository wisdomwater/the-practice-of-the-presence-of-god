## Day 4 - The Promise Hidden in Winter

There are seasons in life when growth seems almost impossible to recognize. We may find ourselves caught in familiar struggles, uncertain whether our efforts are producing any meaningful change. Like a tree standing bare in the middle of winter, our lives may appear still and unfruitful, giving little indication of what is happening beneath the surface. During these seasons, it can become difficult to trust that anything new is taking shape within us.

At eighteen, Brother Lawrence encountered a leafless tree during winter and began contemplating the mystery of its renewal. He understood that although its branches appeared lifeless, spring would eventually bring new leaves, blossoms, and fruit. This simple observation awakened within him a profound awareness of God's providence and power, filling him with a love that would shape the remainder of his life. What others might have passed without noticing became, for him, an opening into the mystery of divine care.

We often associate spiritual growth with visible progress, imagining that transformation should be accompanied by increasing confidence, peace, or understanding. Yet the natural world reminds us that much of life's development occurs beyond our immediate perception. Roots deepen in darkness, seeds germinate beneath the soil, and trees conserve their strength through seasons when outward growth has ceased.

Perhaps our spiritual lives follow a similar rhythm. We may not always recognize the quiet ways that patience, compassion, and trust are developing within us, particularly when circumstances seem unchanged. Brother Lawrence's winter tree invites us to remain attentive to the possibility that divine love continues its work even when there is little visible evidence of renewal.

**Practice**

Find a moment today to observe something in the natural world. It might be a tree outside your window, a plant growing beside a sidewalk, or the movement of clouds across the sky. Spend a few moments noticing what is present without trying to turn the experience into a lesson.

Consider the unseen processes that sustain life and the ways change unfolds gradually, often beyond our awareness. If you are experiencing a season of uncertainty or discouragement, allow this observation to become a gentle reminder that growth does not always announce itself. You can simply remain open to what is unfolding.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Breathing in:** *Your love sustains me.*
> **Breathing out:** *I trust the unseen growth.*
